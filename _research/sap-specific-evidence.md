## 0. Tooling disclosure (read first)

- No general web-search tool was available. Evidence was gathered by fetching specific pages. Open-web searches via scraped search engines returned junk and were abandoned.
- Readable: news.sap.com (including its WordPress REST API for search and full text), help.sap.com (via its internal search and page-content endpoints), the SAP-docs GitHub repositories (raw markdown of the SAP Help Portal sources), roadmaps.sap.com (reachable, not mined).
- BLOCKED (HTTP 403 or "Access Denied"): www.sap.com (all product, pricing and customer-story pages), community.sap.com, me.sap.com SAP Notes (login). The Discovery Center is a JavaScript app and returned no content. IDC and Forrester pages were not retrievable.
- Consequence: SAP customer-story pages (Bosch, LC Waikiki, PwC, Aeropuertos Argentina) were NOT opened. Their figures are taken from SAP News Center articles that summarise them. Per-token and per-AI-unit prices, SAP Notes 3571857 and 3437766, and the SAP AI Services List are UNVERIFIED (not opened).
- Almost all quantified customer evidence is SAP-authored. There is NO independent audit of any figure below. No Forrester TEI or IDC study specific to Joule or SAP Business AI was found (see section 5).

Label key: VENDOR-CLAIMED = SAP or partner marketing. CUSTOMER-REPORTED = a named customer executive quoted by SAP (still published by SAP, not audited). ANALYST/INDEPENDENT = third-party authored.
